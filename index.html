Where My Feet Have Touched the Land

I love traveling and learning. Backpacking around the world with friends and family has shaped how I view history, culture, and architecture.

Explore Interactive Map
🇲🇾 Malaysia

Taman Negara

Trekking through one of the oldest rainforests in the world. Memorable moments include getting a leech on my foot deep in the jungle and waking up in the dead of night to find an entire swarm of black bugs guarding the outhouse. A night I'll never forget.

🇹🇭 Thailand

Ayutthaya Kingdom

Bypassed the typical full-moon party beaches to explore Thailand’s ancient roots. Walking through the magnificent temples and ruins of Ayutthaya leaves you in genuine awe of the engineering and history from centuries past.

🇲🇾 Malaysia

Batu Caves

A 400-million-year-old limestone hill turned sacred shrine. I vividly remember powering through the steep climb up the colorful steps under the intense tropical heat, pausing constantly for water before reaching the temple inside.

🇲🇾 Malaysia

Cameron Highlands Tea Gardens

Lush, rolling tea plantations as far as the eye can see. While visiting, I dropped by a local Sikh temple and shared Langar with the priest—a beautiful reminder that their doors are always open to everyone.

🇮🇹 Italy

Ponte Vecchio, Florence

A historic bridge that has survived fires and reconstructions across centuries. Late one night while wandering through Florence, I sat by the bridge sharing conversations with local travelers about the surrounding Italian architecture.

🇩🇰 Denmark

Copenhagen Canals

The best way to see the city is simply getting lost on a bicycle. Navigating the waterfronts, passing vibrant harbor boats, and taking in the coastal color palette made for an unforgettable ride.

🇹🇳 Tunisia

Amphitheatre of El Jem

At first glance, it feels like a classic Roman gladiatorial arena, but exploring the intricate layout of rooms and vaulted roofing makes it feel like an ancient center of learning or library.

🇯🇵 Japan

Himeji Castle

An extraordinary fortress surrounded by tranquil Zen gardens. I was fascinated by the meticulous tree care methods—scaffolds of wooden props delicately supporting ancient branches to preserve their natural grace.

🇯🇵 Japan

Mount Koya (Koyasan)

Rode a cable car high up into the freezing mountain air. I was fortunate to be welcomed by Buddhist monks to participate in their morning rituals and training—a powerful lesson in meditation, discipline, and mind-body connection.
